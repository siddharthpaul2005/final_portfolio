A retrieval-augmented generation pipeline for personalised learning assistance.

## Key Features

- **Ingestion:** Course documents are chunked and embedded with a text-embedding model; vectors are stored locally.
- **Retrieval:** Relevant chunks are found by cosine similarity and injected into the LLM's context, keeping answers grounded and reducing hallucination.
- **Source attribution:** Answers cite the course material they came from.
- **No fine-tuning:** Draws on course-specific material dynamically, without the cost of fine-tuning a model.
