A stock price and market-movement predictor that supports analysing multiple stocks side by side.

## Key Features

- **Multi-stock:** Select several stocks dynamically and view their movements and predictions together.
- **Models:** Linear Regression, Random Forest, and a basic LSTM prototype for sequence modelling.
- **Feature engineering:** Lag features, rolling statistics and volume-weighted price deltas.
- **Data:** Historical prices pulled with yFinance and processed with Pandas.

## Reflections & Learnings

- Building intuition for the signal-to-noise ratio in financial time series, and why price prediction is so hard.
