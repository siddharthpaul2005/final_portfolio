A notebook that predicts next-day stock moves for any set of tickers from historical price data, framed both as classification and as regression.

## Key Features

- **Any tickers, any range:** Pick the stocks and the date range, and the data is pulled with yFinance.
- **Technical indicators:** 20/50-day SMAs and EMAs, RSI and Bollinger Bands as features.
- **Classification:** Will tomorrow close higher or lower? Logistic Regression and Random Forest, compared on accuracy, precision, recall and F1 in a per-ticker performance table.
- **Regression:** Predicts tomorrow's closing price with Linear Regression and a Random Forest Regressor, scored with R², MAE and RMSE, plus actual-vs-predicted plots.
- **Feature importance:** Which indicators actually drove the models' decisions.

## Reflections & Learnings

- Direction (classification) is easier to interpret, while price level (regression) carries more information, but a small error can flip the trading decision.
- Building intuition for how noisy daily financial time series are, which later shaped the far more rigorous pairs trading study at Echelon.
