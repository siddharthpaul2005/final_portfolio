**AIDA: Artificial Intelligence, Derived Not Assumed** is my blog and knowledge repository. It treats machine learning as a mathematical discipline rather than a collection of black-box algorithms: every model is derived from first principles before it's built.

## Key Features

- **A five-stage pathway:**
  - **Mathematics:** linear algebra, calculus, probability
  - **Machine Learning:** convex optimisation, empirical risk minimisation, kernels
  - **Deep Learning:** backprop, CNNs, non-convex optimisation
  - **Modern ML:** self-attention, diffusion, LLMs
  - **Research:** generalisation guarantees and frontiers
- **Formal proofs:** Theorems written out in LaTeX before any code.
- **PyTorch from scratch:** Each idea is implemented after it's derived.
- **Interactive knowledge graph:** Shows how the concepts connect.
- **Live:** Published at aida-derive.vercel.app, with blogs, a mathematics section, projects and a research archive.
