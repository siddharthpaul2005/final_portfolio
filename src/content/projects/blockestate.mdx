A decentralised real estate platform on Solana that enables smart-contract-based property transactions without intermediaries.

## Key Features

- **On-chain listings:** Properties are represented as on-chain program accounts, and ownership transfers are recorded directly on the ledger.
- **Rust smart contracts:** Solana programs enforce the full transaction lifecycle, from listing to escrow to transfer of title.
- **React frontend:** Browse listings and transact from the browser.

## Reflections & Learnings

- A deep dive into Solana's account-based architecture, program-derived addresses (PDAs) and the Sealevel parallel execution environment.
- Rust's memory-safety guarantees in the context of on-chain programs.
